<h2>Enterprise IT Asset Management (ITAM) & Hardware Lifecycle</h2><p>Centralized IT hardware inventory tracking across all corporate assets from initial procurement through end-of-life disposal.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Hardware Serial & Barcode Indexing:</strong> Cataloged all server, switch, laptop, and display serial numbers into a searchable asset database.</li><li><strong>Warranty Expiration Alerting:</strong> Configured automated alerts notifying IT management 90 days prior to vendor hardware support warranty expiration.</li><li><strong>Asset Custody Tracking:</strong> Standardized digital sign-off procedures linking specific endpoint device serial numbers to assigned staff users.</li></ul>
