<h2>Interactive Transparent OLED Touch Cabinet Infrastructure</h2><p>Architected physical showcase cabinets featuring front-facing transparent OLED touch displays that overlay digital blueprints over internal hardware.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Precise Graphical Overlay Alignment:</strong> Aligned digital UI blueprints to match the exact millimeter position of physical internal components.</li><li><strong>Touch-Triggered Internal Spotlighting:</strong> Programmed localized LED interior spotlights to illuminate specific physical modules upon user touch.</li><li><strong>Low-Power Idle Timeout Modes:</strong> Configured automatic screen dimming and lighting power savings when no venue visitors are present.</li></ul>
