<h2>Enterprise Mailbox Archiving & Legal Hold Governance</h2><p>Implemented immutable cloud mailbox retention policies to preserve corporate email communications for legal compliance and eDiscovery.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Litigation Hold Deployment:</strong> Applied Litigation Hold to executive mailboxes, ensuring deleted items are permanently preserved in hidden hold stores.</li><li><strong>Auto-Expanding Archive Mailboxes:</strong> Enabled M365 Auto-Expanding Archiving providing unlimited archive storage for heavy email users.</li><li><strong>Content Search & eDiscovery:</strong> Configured Purview eDiscovery manager roles allowing compliance officers to perform targeted keyword searches across held mailboxes.</li></ul>
