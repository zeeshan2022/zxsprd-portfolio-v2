<h2>Enterprise Mail Flow Inspection & Spam Mitigation</h2><p>Designed mail transfer agent (MTA) filtering rules and bounce log analytics to improve outbound email deliverability and stop incoming spoofing attempts.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>DKIM & DMARC Standardization:</strong> Enforced cryptographic signing on outbound automated payroll and transactional mail systems.</li><li><strong>Rejected Mail Flow Analytics:</strong> Built automated Exim log parsing scripts to flag non-delivery reports (NDRs) and invalid HELO rejection patterns.</li><li><strong>Spam Score Tuning:</strong> Fine-tuned inbound spam detection rules to eliminate credential phishing emails.</li></ul>
