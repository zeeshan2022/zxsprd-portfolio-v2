<h2>Enterprise Wireless Network Heatmapping & RF Survey</h2><p>Optimized enterprise Wi-Fi coverage across multi-floor corporate offices to eliminate dead zones and support seamless voice roaming.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>RF Spectrum Analysis:</strong> Identified external co-channel interference sources and non-Wi-Fi radio frequency noise.</li><li><strong>Roaming Handoff Optimization:</strong> Calibrated 802.11k/v/r fast-roaming parameters to ensure uninterrupted VoIP calls while moving between floors.</li><li><strong>Tx Power & Channel Tuning:</strong> Adjusted AP radio transmit power and channel widths (20MHz/40MHz) to minimize adjacent channel interference.</li></ul>
