<h2>Healthcare IT Infrastructure Security & Compliance Framework</h2><p>Engineered data security controls and infrastructure access standards for healthcare management platforms to comply with strict patient data privacy mandates.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):</strong> Standardized least-privilege administrative access models across electronic health record (EHR) databases.</li><li><strong>Data In-Transit & At-Rest Encryption:</strong> Standardized AES-256 block storage encryption and TLS 1.3 enforcement across medical data transmission routes.</li><li><strong>Audit Logging & Traceability:</strong> Configured detailed user activity audit trails to support regulatory compliance verifications.</li></ul>
