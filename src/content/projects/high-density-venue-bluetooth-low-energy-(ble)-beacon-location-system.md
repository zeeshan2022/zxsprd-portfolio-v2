<h2>High-Density Venue Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Beacon Location System</h2><p>Architected indoor micro-location beacon networks providing sub-meter indoor navigation and contextual exhibit notifications to mobile app users.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Strategic BLE Beacon Grid Deployment:</strong> Calibrated signal power and placement for 100+ battery and USB BLE beacons across large venue halls.</li><li><strong>Indoor Trilateration Wayfinding:</strong> Calculated real-time mobile device positions using multi-beacon signal strength (RSSI) trilateration algorithms.</li><li><strong>Contextual Proximity Triggers:</strong> Pushed location-specific exhibit audio guides and promotional info to user phones as they approached target exhibits.</li></ul>
