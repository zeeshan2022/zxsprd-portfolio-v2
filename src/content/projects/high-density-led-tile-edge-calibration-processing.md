<h2>High-Density LED Tile Edge Calibration & Processing</h2><p>Executed fine-pitch indoor LED display tile calibration to ensure seamless multi-panel visual continuity and accurate color space reproduction.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Pixel-Level Chroma Calibration:</strong> Utilized specialized optical calibration cameras measuring and adjusting individual LED diode brightness and color tint.</li><li><strong>Hardware Edge Seam Removal:</strong> Adjusted brightness coefficient values along LED panel boundaries to eliminate dark vertical and horizontal seam lines.</li><li><strong>HDR10 Color Mapping:</strong> Programmed LED video processors to support 10-bit HDR content rendering with expanded contrast ratios.</li></ul>
