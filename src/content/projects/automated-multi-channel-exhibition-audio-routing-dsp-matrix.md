<h2>Automated Multi-Channel Exhibition Audio Routing & DSP Matrix</h2><p>Architected venue-wide digital audio routing systems distributing background music, localized exhibit audio, and emergency announcements seamlessly.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Centralized Q-SYS Audio DSP:</strong> Programmed custom audio signal paths, volume ducking rules, and parametric equalizers across 32 independent output zones.</li><li><strong>Emergency Public Address Override:</strong> Integrated building safety contacts to mute all exhibition entertainment audio instantly during fire alarm triggers.</li><li><strong>Automated Ambient Noise Compensation:</strong> Deployed ambient sensing microphones automatically adjusting exhibit volume based on crowds' ambient noise levels.</li></ul>
