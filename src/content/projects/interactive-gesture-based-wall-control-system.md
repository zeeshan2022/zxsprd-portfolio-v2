<h2>Interactive Gesture-Based Wall Control System</h2><p>Architected touchless interactive video installations utilizing infrared depth cameras to translate audience body movements into dynamic visual art.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Depth-Camera Body Tracking:</strong> Programmed optical depth sensors to track user skeletal joints and hand gestures in variable lighting conditions.</li><li><strong>Real-Time Particle Physics Engine:</strong> Rendered 60 FPS interactive GPU particle fields responding fluidly to visitor proximity and movement vectors.</li><li><strong>Multi-User Tracking Scoping:</strong> Filtered depth noise and prioritized tracking inputs for up to 6 simultaneous venue visitors.</li></ul>
