<h2>Interactive Digital Signature Wall & Document Archival Infrastructure</h2><p>Engineered interactive VIP digital guestbook terminals capturing stylus signatures and immediately rendering them on dynamic exhibit screens.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Sub-Millimeter Stylus Pressure Tracking:</strong> Captured high-precision touch stylus inputs preserving natural handwriting thickness and signature curves.</li><li><strong>Instant WebGL Signature Particle Effects:</strong> Rendered newly captured signatures as floating glowing light particles moving across main venue display screens.</li><li><strong>Automated PDF Guestbook Export:</strong> Compiled collected signatures and guest messages automatically into high-resolution archival PDF books.</li></ul>
