<h2>Dynamic DMX Kinetic Ceiling Sculpture Control System</h2><p>Architected kinetic ceiling installations synchronizing motorized winch movement and internal LED color changes to create physical 3D wave forms.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>High-Speed Art-Net Routing:</strong> Broadcasted multi-universe Art-Net lighting data over Ethernet switches to motor winch controllers.</li><li><strong>Frame-Locked Audio Sync:</strong> Locked kinetic ball height trajectories to soundtrack timestamps using precise MIDI clock pulses.</li><li><strong>Hardware Safety E-Stop Interlocks:</strong> Integrated physical emergency stop relays cutting motor power instantly upon fault detection.</li></ul>
