<h2>Corporate Showroom Smart Automation & Crestron AV Matrix</h2><p>Designed centralized AV-over-IP matrix distribution and environmental automation systems for executive corporate experience centers.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>DM-NVX AV-over-IP Routing:</strong> Distributed 4K video feeds over 10GbE network switches to any showroom display screen with sub-frame latency.</li><li><strong>One-Touch Scene Presets:</strong> Programmed tablet interfaces enabling staff to trigger coordinated lighting, audio, and video presentation modes.</li><li><strong>Building Management Integration:</strong> Tied AV control systems into facility BACnet protocols for automated environmental power scheduling.</li></ul>
