<h2>Interactive E-Poster Digital Exhibition Display System</h2><p>Engineered interactive digital poster terminals replacing traditional paper poster boards at international medical and scientific conventions.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>High-Resolution PDF Rendering:</strong> Integrated GPU-accelerated PDF viewers allowing smooth pinch-to-zoom exploration of high-density research charts.</li><li><strong>Keyword & Author Search Engine:</strong> Built fast search interfaces allowing conference attendees to filter hundreds of e-posters by subject or presenter name.</li><li><strong>Interactive Author Contact Messaging:</strong> Enabled attendees to send direct message inquiries or request paper downloads straight from kiosk screens.</li></ul>
