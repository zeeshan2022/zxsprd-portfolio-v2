<h2>M365 Purview Data Loss Prevention (DLP) & Governance</h2><p>Implemented Microsoft Purview data loss prevention and retention policies to prevent unauthorized sharing of corporate assets and sensitive records.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Data Classification Rules:</strong> Formulated auto-labeling policies to identify and tag sensitive financial and personal identification information.</li><li><strong>Exchange & Teams DLP:</strong> Enforced transport rules blocking external auto-forwarding and unauthorized document attachments.</li><li><strong>Audit & Incident Alerts:</strong> Configured real-time compliance administrator alerts for unauthorized policy breach attempts.</li></ul>
