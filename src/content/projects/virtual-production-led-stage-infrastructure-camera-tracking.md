<h2>Virtual Production LED Stage Infrastructure & Camera Tracking</h2><p>Architected low-latency media server clusters driving real-time 3D virtual environments onto LED stage walls synchronized with cinema camera tracking.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>nDisplay Multi-GPU Cluster Rendering:</strong> Synchronized multiple graphics rendering nodes driving high-resolution LED backdrop walls.</li><li><strong>Optical Camera Tracking Integration:</strong> Bound sub-millimeter camera tracking data to Unreal Engine virtual cameras for real-time background perspective rendering.</li><li><strong>Tri-Level Genlock Synchronization:</strong> Locked camera shutter frequencies, LED processing cards, and render node GPUs to eliminate rolling shutter tearing.</li></ul>
