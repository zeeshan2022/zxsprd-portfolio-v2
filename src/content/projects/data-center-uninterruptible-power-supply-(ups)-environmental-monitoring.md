<h2>Data Center Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) & Environmental Monitoring</h2><p>Designed power redundancy and environmental threshold alert systems to prevent server hardware failure during utility power outages.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Dual-PDU Power Redundancy:</strong> Configured dual A/B feed power distribution units (PDUs) to support multi-corded server power supplies.</li><li><strong>SNMP Environmental Monitoring:</strong> Deployed network-attached environmental probes tracking temperature, humidity, and liquid detection.</li><li><strong>Automated Graceful Shutdown:</strong> Configured automated server shutdown agents to gracefully power down critical VMs before battery depletion.</li></ul>
