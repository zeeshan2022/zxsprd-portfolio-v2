<h2>Large-Scale Transparent LED Glass Wall Deployment</h2><p>Engineered architectural transparent LED glass installations displaying vibrant video content while preserving interior natural daylighting.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>75% Transparency LED Mesh:</strong> Installed high-density transparent LED strip panels mounted directly behind building exterior glass.</li><li><strong>Novastar Fiber Video Signal Distribution:</strong> Routed uncompressed 4K video signals across optical fiber extenders to LED receiving cards.</li><li><strong>Ambient Light Sensor Auto-Dimming:</strong> Programmed optical sensors dynamically adjusting screen brightness from 6000 nits daylight to 500 nits nighttime.</li></ul>
