<h2>Interactive Augmented Reality (AR) Photo Booth Kiosk</h2><p>Deployed self-service photo booth kiosks enabling event attendees to pose with dynamic 3D branded props and instantly receive digital photos.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Real-Time 3D Face & Body Tracking:</strong> Integrated high-speed computer vision SDKs tracking user facial landmarks for precise 3D prop positioning.</li><li><strong>DSLR Camera Automation:</strong> Programmed direct USB tethering controls capturing studio-quality high-resolution photographs automatically.</li><li><strong>Instant Cloud Upload & QR Delivery:</strong> Uploaded processed branded photo outputs to AWS S3, generating instant mobile QR scan download links.</li></ul>
