<h2>Healthcare Management System Security Architecture</h2><p>Implemented role-based access control and encrypted communication protocols for healthcare management platforms.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Least-Privilege Access Control:</strong> Enforced fine-grained RBAC permissions for clinical staff accessing medical databases.</li><li><strong>Data Encryption:</strong> Standardized TLS 1.3 encryption in-transit and AES-256 encryption at-rest for patient records.</li><li><strong>Audit Logging:</strong> Configured comprehensive log retention pipelines tracking user record access.</li></ul>
