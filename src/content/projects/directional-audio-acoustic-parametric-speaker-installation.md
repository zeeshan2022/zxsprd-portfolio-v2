<h2>Directional Audio & Acoustic Parametric Speaker Installation</h2><p>Engineered highly targeted acoustic zones in crowded museum exhibits using narrow-beam ultrasonic parametric speakers.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Ultrasonic Sound Isolation:</strong> Focused localized audio beams strictly onto 1-meter floor target spots, eliminating cross-exhibit sound bleed.</li><li><strong>Motion-Triggered Playback:</strong> Integrated proximity floor sensors triggering exhibit narrative audio only when a visitor entered the beam zone.</li><li><strong>Dante Digital Audio Network:</strong> Routed multi-channel digital audio over IP networks to central DSP processing racks.</li></ul>
