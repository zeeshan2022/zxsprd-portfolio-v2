<h2>Multi-Tenant Cloud Tenant Partitioning & Billing Management</h2><p>Engineered a structured resource tagging schema to track cloud infrastructure expenditure and allocate operational costs to specific business units.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Mandatory Cost-Center Tagging:</strong> Enforced OCI tag defaults automatically tagging newly provisioned compute, storage, and networking resources.</li><li><strong>Automated Budget Threshold Alerts:</strong> Configured real-time email alerts when monthly departmental spend exceeded 80% and 100% forecast thresholds.</li><li><strong>License Quota Harvesting:</strong> Built automated license cleanup scripts to identify and reclaim inactive M365 licenses monthly.</li></ul>
