<h2>Interactive Dynamic Projection Mapping Showroom Controller</h2><p>Architected wireless tablet control systems enabling interactive selection of projection mapping presets onto 3D physical models in showrooms.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Low-Latency UDP OSC Messaging:</strong> Transmitted instant wireless control signals from administrative tablets to projection mapping servers.</li><li><strong>Seamless Video Preset Transitions:</strong> Configured cross-fading media playout servers allowing fluid visual transitions between projection themes.</li><li><strong>Interactive Component Highlight Mode:</strong> Enabled staff to select individual product features on the tablet to project targeted highlights onto the physical model.</li></ul>
