<h2>Real-Time Experiential Venue Visitor Analytics Network</h2><p>Engineered anonymized optical tracking networks measuring crowd density, popular movement pathways, and average dwell time per exhibit.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Anonymized Spatial Tracking:</strong> Processed overhead camera feeds converting human movements into anonymous x/y coordinate data points without storing video.</li><li><strong>Zone Dwell-Time Calculation:</strong> Aggregated visitor dwell-time metrics across specific exhibit zones to identify top performing interactive installations.</li><li><strong>Live Heatmap Visualization:</strong> Rendered real-time crowd heatmaps on operations management dashboards to optimize venue staff positioning.</li></ul>
