<h2>Retail Experiential Smart Mirror & RFID Product Scanner</h2><p>Engineered interactive smart mirror technologies triggering instant product information, sizing options, and styling recommendations upon garment detection.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Sub-Second RFID Tag Detection:</strong> Integrated near-field RFID reader antennas behind fitting room mirrors to trigger immediate content changes.</li><li><strong>Dynamic Fitting Room UI:</strong> Developed touch-responsive mirror display interfaces allowing customers to request different sizes directly from store staff.</li><li><strong>Store Inventory API Bridge:</strong> Connected smart mirror terminals to local store inventory databases for real-time stock availability verification.</li></ul>
