<h2>Enterprise Network Bandwidth Optimization & Quality of Service (QoS)</h2><p>Implemented perimeter traffic shaping policies to guarantee low-latency bandwidth allocations for critical voice and ERP data traffic.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>VoIP & Video Prioritization:</strong> Configured DSCP EF (Expedited Forwarding) queues for SIP/RTP voice traffic across perimeter WAN links.</li><li><strong>Non-Essential Rate Limiting:</strong> Enforced maximum bandwidth caps on cloud storage synchronization and personal media streaming categories.</li><li><strong>Guaranteed Bandwidth Pools:</strong> Reserved minimum guaranteed throughput pipes for core database replication traffic during peak operational hours.</li></ul>
