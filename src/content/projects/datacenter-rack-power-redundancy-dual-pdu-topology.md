<h2>Datacenter Rack Power Redundancy & Dual-PDU Topology</h2><p>Architected zero-SPOF server rack power topologies ensuring dual-corded servers remain powered during single UPS or utility circuit failures.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>A/B Circuit Separation:</strong> Connected Server Power Supply 1 to PDU-A (UPS-1) and Server Power Supply 2 to PDU-B (UPS-2).</li><li><strong>Simulated Circuit Outage Testing:</strong> Conducted manual breaker cutover tests verifying seamless continuous server operation during single PDU power loss.</li><li><strong>Smart PDU Load Monitoring:</strong> Configured PDU SNMP monitoring ensuring total power draw on single feeds remained under 50% capacity to handle failover loads.</li></ul>
