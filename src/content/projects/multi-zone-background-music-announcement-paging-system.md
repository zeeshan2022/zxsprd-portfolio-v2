<h2>Multi-Zone Background Music & Announcement Paging System</h2><p>Architected IP-networked paging and background music infrastructure delivering clear voice announcements and audio zoning across venue facilities.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>SIP Paging Microphone Integration:</strong> Enabled staff to make targeted live or pre-recorded voice announcements to specific zones using desk IP phones.</li><li><strong>Automated Priority Ducking:</strong> Programmed DSP mixers to automatically lower background music volume whenever voice announcements are triggered.</li><li><strong>Multi-Source Streaming Media:</strong> Configured independent streaming audio channels playing localized background ambient music tailored to specific exhibit areas.</li></ul>
