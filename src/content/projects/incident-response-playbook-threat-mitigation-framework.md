<h2>Incident Response Playbook & Threat Mitigation Framework</h2><p>Authored and operationalized the corporate Incident Response Playbook (BSH-SEC-PLB-001) to standardize threat detection, triage, containment, and forensic investigation.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>6-Phase Incident Lifecycle:</strong> Standardized Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, and Lessons Learned protocols following NIST/ISO 27001 baselines.</li><li><strong>Forensic Analysis & Triage:</strong> Integrated forensic log investigation workflows using CyberChef and SIEM aggregators for fast threat hunting.</li><li><strong>Isolation Protocols:</strong> Established operational technology (OT) and subnetwork isolation playbooks to stop lateral malware movement.</li></ul>
