<h2>Datacenter Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) Battery Testing Automation</h2><p>Automated periodic UPS battery health diagnostics to detect declining runtime capacities prior to real utility power failures.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Scheduled Monthly Battery Self-Tests:</strong> Configured automated off-peak self-test routines verifying battery cell internal resistance and voltage stability.</li><li><strong>Runtime Capacity Alerting:</strong> Set up SNMP alerts notifying engineers if estimated battery backup runtime dropped below 30 minutes at full load.</li><li><strong>Proactive Battery Replacement Schedules:</strong> Tracked battery install dates and internal impedance metrics to schedule replacement before end-of-life failure.</li></ul>
