<h2>High-Lumen Laser Projector Network Control & Calibration</h2><p>Centralized network management and automated operational scheduling for multi-projector display arrays in exhibition centers.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>PJLink Unified IP Control:</strong> Standardized network power, input switching, and shutter control commands using PJLink open protocol baselines.</li><li><strong>Automated Shutter & Power Timers:</strong> Programmed automated daily power cycles to preserve laser diode longevity and reduce venue energy usage.</li><li><strong>Thermal & Filter Health Monitoring:</strong> Polled real-time projector intake temperatures and fan speeds to schedule preventive filter cleanings.</li></ul>
