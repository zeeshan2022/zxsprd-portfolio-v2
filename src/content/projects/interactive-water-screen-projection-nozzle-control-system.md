<h2>Interactive Water Screen Projection & Nozzle Control System</h2><p>Architected outdoor water screen projection systems creating semi-transparent mist curtains illuminated by high-lumen laser projectors.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>High-Pressure Mist Generation:</strong> Controlled high-volume water pump manifolds creating dense, uniform water curtains resilient against wind gusts.</li><li><strong>Rear Projection Laser Alignment:</strong> Positioned 30,000-lumen laser projectors behind mist screens to maximize visual contrast and saturation.</li><li><strong>Solenoid Water Gate Control:</strong> Programmed DMX solenoid valves triggering instant water screen activation only during scheduled show times.</li></ul>
