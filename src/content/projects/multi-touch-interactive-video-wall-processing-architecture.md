<h2>Multi-Touch Interactive Video Wall Processing Architecture</h2><p>Configured hardware processing pipelines capable of driving 16K resolution video walls with zero-latency multi-user touch feedback.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Hardware Video Wall Processing:</strong> Integrated Datapath display controllers to split high-resolution canvas outputs across multiple 4K panels.</li><li><strong>LiDAR Laser Touch Tracking:</strong> Calibrated perimeter laser sensors to convert multi-user physical gestures into precise desktop mouse inputs.</li><li><strong>EDID Management:</strong> Standardized display timing and handshake signals across video extenders to eliminate screen flickering.</li></ul>
