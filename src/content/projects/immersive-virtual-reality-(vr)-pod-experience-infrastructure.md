<h2>Immersive Virtual Reality (VR) Pod Experience Infrastructure</h2><p>Architected hardware and motion-seat synchronization infrastructure powering commercial virtual reality simulation pods.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Motion Telemetry Sync:</strong> Extracted real-time vehicle telemetry vectors from VR applications to drive physical 3-DOF motion platform actuators.</li><li><strong>Overhead Spring Cable Retraction:</strong> Installed ceiling spring retractor systems removing cable tension and preventing VR headset wire tangling.</li><li><strong>External Spectator Displays:</strong> Mirrored VR headset eye buffers onto external 4K monitors allowing waiting venue queues to watch live gameplay.</li></ul>
