<h2>Multi-Touch Interactive Wall Floor-to-Ceiling Canvas</h2><p>Engineered large-format interactive wall canvas systems combining multi-projector optical blending with perimeter laser tracking sensors.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>LiDAR Multi-Point Touch Tracking:</strong> Calibrated perimeter infrared laser sensors to track up to 30 simultaneous user touch coordinates across a 10-meter canvas.</li><li><strong>Seamless Multi-Projector Blending:</strong> Applied optical edge-blending and warping algorithms uniting 4 high-lumen laser projectors into a unified high-resolution display.</li><li><strong>Interactive Visual Particle Engine:</strong> Rendered zero-latency 60 FPS visual fluid dynamics responding fluidly to visitor physical interactions.</li></ul>
