<h2>Cybersecurity Risk Register & Impact Assessment Matrix</h2><p>Established a formal IT risk management framework cataloging technical vulnerabilities, business impact scores, and mitigation action plans.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Likelihood vs Impact Scoring Matrix:</strong> Evaluated operational, financial, and reputational risk factors to assign standardized risk ratings (Low to Critical).</li><li><strong>Mitigation Ownership Tracking:</strong> Assigned specific technical remediations and target completion dates to system administrators.</li><li><strong>Executive Board Reporting:</strong> Prepared quarterly residual risk summaries for executive management review.</li></ul>
