<h2>Augmented Reality (AR) Smart Display Stand Architecture</h2><p>Architected transparent display showcase boxes blending physical product displays with dynamic augmented reality digital animations.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Transparent OLED Integration:</strong> Calibrated backlight illumination and transparent screen opacity to layer digital UI graphics over physical items.</li><li><strong>Interactive Physical Lighting:</strong> Synchronized internal LED spot-lighting relays with digital screen video events to highlight specific product components.</li><li><strong>User Touch Interactivity:</strong> Embedded capacitive touch overlays on display glass allowing visitors to rotate virtual 3D product cross-sections.</li></ul>
