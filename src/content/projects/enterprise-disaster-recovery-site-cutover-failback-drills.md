<h2>Enterprise Disaster Recovery Site Cutover & Failback Drills</h2><p>Organized structured disaster recovery testing drills to measure system resilience and ensure operational continuity during catastrophic outages.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Automated Cutover Execution:</strong> Tested database Data Guard failover and virtual machine replica spin-up under simulated blackout conditions.</li><li><strong>DNS & IP Rerouting:</strong> Validated automated internal and external DNS record updates redirecting client traffic to the secondary DR datacenter.</li><li><strong>RPO/RTO Validation:</strong> Achieved an RPO under 5 minutes and an RTO under 1 hour for all Tier-1 transactional services.</li></ul>
