<h2>Interactive Touchscreen Kiosk Hardware Thermal Management</h2><p>Architected physical thermal cooling and environmental protection solutions for outdoor interactive touchscreen kiosks operating in high ambient heat.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Forced-Air Thermal Ducting:</strong> Designed internal fan ducting isolating intake and exhaust airflow channels to dissipate internal display and PC heat.</li><li><strong>Variable-Speed Temperature Control:</strong> Programmed industrial thermostats adjusting fan speeds dynamically based on internal enclosure temperatures.</li><li><strong>Automated Emergency Thermal Cutoff:</strong> Integrated hardware safety relays isolating power to displays if internal temperatures crossed 55°C.</li></ul>
