<h2>Multi-Site Site-to-Site IPsec VPN Network Deployment</h2><p>Architected encrypted WAN connectivity connecting branch office networks to central datacenter services.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>AES-256 Encryption:</strong> Enforced AES-256 encryption and SHA-256 authentication hash baselines on VPN tunnels.</li><li><strong>Redundant Tunneling:</strong> Configured backup VPN paths over secondary ISP links for automatic failover.</li><li><strong>Centralized Access Control:</strong> Restricted inter-site traffic to specific required server IPs and ports.</li></ul>
