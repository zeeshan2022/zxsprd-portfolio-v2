<h2>SIEM & Centralized Firewall Syslog Aggregation</h2><p>Deployed a dedicated Syslog server infrastructure to aggregate and index security event logs from enterprise FortiGate firewalls and core switches.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Centralized Log Ingestion:</strong> Standardized Syslog forwarding protocols from perimeter firewalls to an isolated log collector appliance.</li><li><strong>SIEM Integration & Parsing:</strong> Configured log parsing rules and automated alerts for brute-force attacks, policy violations, and anomaly detection.</li><li><strong>Retention & Compliance:</strong> Established automated log compression, rotation, and encrypted long-term storage to meet ISO/UAE compliance.</li></ul>
