<h2>Interactive Augmented Reality (AR) Retail Window Display</h2><p>Architected street-facing storefront glass installations using optical depth sensors to track outdoor pedestrian movement and render instant 3D apparel overlays.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>High-Brightness Daylight Screen Deployment:</strong> Utilized 4,000-nit high-brightness commercial displays maintaining visual clarity through direct sunlight.</li><li><strong>Real-Time Outdoor Pose Estimation:</strong> Filtered outdoor sunlight noise to track pedestrian body joints and fit virtual 3D clothing items in mid-stride.</li><li><strong>Automated QR Code Conversion:</strong> Displayed instant mobile QR codes allowing interested sidewalk shoppers to buy featured items directly online.</li></ul>
