<h2>Experiential Venue Power Sequence Automation & Relay Control</h2><p>Architected automated power distribution sequences ensuring hundreds of high-draw venue displays and media servers power on safely in staged phases.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Sequential Staged Power-On:</strong> Programmed delayed relay switching sequences preventing massive electrical inrush current spikes during venue startup.</li><li><strong>Scheduled Automated Shutdowns:</strong> Executed graceful operating system shutdowns on media servers before cutting main relay power circuits at night.</li><li><strong>Remote Hard Reset Web Panel:</strong> Built password-protected web interface allowing technicians to power-cycle individual frozen exhibit screens remotely.</li></ul>
