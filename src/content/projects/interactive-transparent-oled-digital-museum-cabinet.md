<h2>Interactive transparent OLED Digital Museum Cabinet</h2><p>Designed luxury museum artifact showcases featuring front-facing transparent OLED touch displays that present dynamic historical context layers over physical artifacts.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Precise Graphical Overlay Alignment:</strong> Aligned 3D digital UI schematics to match the exact physical dimensions of internal museum items.</li><li><strong>Touch-Triggered Internal Spotlighting:</strong> Programmed localized LED interior spotlights to illuminate specific physical artifact components upon user touch.</li><li><strong>Low-Power Idle Timeout Modes:</strong> Configured automatic screen dimming and lighting power savings when no venue visitors are present.</li></ul>
