<h2>Interactive Kinetic Optical Reflection Wall Engine</h2><p>Designed high-speed kinematic control networks driving physical dual-axis motorized mirror arrays that reflect targeted spotlight beams into fluid visual patterns across exhibition spaces.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>High-Speed Multi-Drop Serial Routing:</strong> Transmitted precise motor angle commands to 100+ dual-axis kinetic mirror controllers over high-speed RS485 serial buses.</li><li><strong>Real-Time Kinematic Wave Algorithms:</strong> Converted incoming Art-Net lighting data into smooth physical motor tilt angles using mathematical acceleration curves.</li><li><strong>Automated Zero-Position Calibration:</strong> Integrated optical homing sensors automatically resetting mirror arrays to baseline zero positions upon system boot.</li></ul>
