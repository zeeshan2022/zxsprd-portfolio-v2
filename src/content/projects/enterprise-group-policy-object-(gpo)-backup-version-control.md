<h2>Enterprise Group Policy Object (GPO) Backup & Version Control</h2><p>Implemented automated backup and change-tracking pipelines for corporate Group Policy Objects to prevent accidental misconfiguration downtime.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Automated Daily GPO Backups:</strong> Built PowerShell scripts backing up all domain GPOs daily to encrypted off-site repositories.</li><li><strong>GPO Change Diff Auditing:</strong> Configured automated scripts parsing XML GPO reports to highlight policy modifications made within 24 hours.</li><li><strong>One-Click Policy Rollback:</strong> Established documented, tested restoration procedures to roll back corrupted or faulty GPOs instantly.</li></ul>
