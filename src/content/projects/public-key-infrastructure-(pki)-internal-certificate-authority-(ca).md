<h2>Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) & Internal Certificate Authority (CA)</h2><p>Deployed an enterprise two-tier PKI architecture to issue, manage, and revoke internal X.509 digital certificates across domain devices.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Two-Tier Hierarchy:</strong> Isolated offline Root Certificate Authority with an online subordinate Issuing CA joined to Active Directory.</li><li><strong>Automated Certificate Auto-Enrollment:</strong> Configured Group Policy auto-enrollment templates for workstation SSL, IPsec, and Wi-Fi authentication.</li><li><strong>CRL & OCSP Revocation Distribution:</strong> Published high-availability Certificate Revocation Lists (CRLs) and Online Responder endpoints.</li></ul>
