<h2>Dynamic Architectural LED Façade DMX Control Architecture</h2><p>Designed building-scale architectural pixel-mapping installations controlling exterior facade LED lighting fixtures for urban landmarks.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>High-Universe sACN Distribution:</strong> Broadcasted 100+ universes of sACN lighting data over gigabit optical fiber networks to field SPI controllers.</li><li><strong>Procedural Pixel-Mapping Software:</strong> Configured MADRIX media servers generating real-time geometric visual patterns across irregular building surfaces.</li><li><strong>Astronomical Clock Scheduling:</strong> Programmed automatic lighting pattern triggers aligned with daily sunset and sunrise times.</li></ul>
