<h2>Interactive Sound-Reactive LED Sculpture Control System</h2><p>Architected real-time audio-reactive lighting installations mapping surrounding musical frequencies directly to 3D LED pixel arrays.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Real-Time FFT Spectrum Decomposition:</strong> Processed incoming audio signals separating bass, midrange, and treble frequencies into distinct lighting triggers.</li><li><strong>Spatial 3D Pixel Mapping:</strong> Mapped thousands of addressable LED nodes in 3D coordinate space to pulse light outward from the sculpture center.</li><li><strong>Dynamic Color Palette Generation:</strong> Programmed automated color transitions shifting color schemes based on music tempo and energy.</li></ul>
