<h2>Immersive Multi-Channel Spatial Audio System Design</h2><p>Designed spatial audio reproduction systems capable of moving virtual sound objects dynamically in 3D space around venue visitors.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Dante Networked Audio Infrastructure:</strong> Transmitted uncompressed 24-bit/96kHz digital audio channels over standard Ethernet switches to amplifiers.</li><li><strong>Q-SYS Spatial Audio Processing:</strong> Programmed central DSP processors executing real-time 3D sound panning and room acoustic equalization.</li><li><strong>Acoustic Isolation Calibration:</strong> Applied acoustic wall treatments and tuned speaker delay matrices to achieve precise spatial sound localization.</li></ul>
