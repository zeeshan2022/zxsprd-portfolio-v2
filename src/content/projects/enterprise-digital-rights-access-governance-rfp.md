<h2>Enterprise Digital Rights & Access Governance RFP</h2><p>Prepared end-to-end technical requirements and governance standards for enterprise-wide EDRM, email encryption, dynamic watermarking, and file tracking implementation.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Granular Rights Control:</strong> Defined enforcement specifications for restricting file printing, editing, screen grabs, and RDP access across desktop and mobile devices.</li><li><strong>Dynamic Watermarking & Revocation:</strong> Mandated dynamic watermarks (user IP, timestamp) and remote instant access revocation for shared documents.</li><li><strong>Outlook & Cloud Integration:</strong> Detailed seamless integration hooks with M365, SharePoint, and corporate email streams.</li></ul>
