<h2>Interactive Motion-Tracking Shadow Wall Installation</h2><p>Created interactive shadow projection exhibits where physical user shadows are captured by vision sensors and transformed into animated digital graphics.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Optical Flow Silhouette Extraction:</strong> Processed real-time camera video feeds to isolate user body silhouettes cleanly from background lighting noise.</li><li><strong>Interactive Physics GPU Shaders:</strong> Applied custom GLSL GPU shaders generating colorful trailing liquid and particle effects originating from user shadows.</li><li><strong>High-Frame-Rate Projection:</strong> Rendered physics visual output at 120Hz to guarantee instantaneous physical shadow movement tracking.</li></ul>
