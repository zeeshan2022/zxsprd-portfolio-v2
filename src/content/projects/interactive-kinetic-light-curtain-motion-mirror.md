<h2>Interactive Kinetic Light Curtain & Motion Mirror</h2><p>Architected 3D spatial light curtains made of hanging LED pixel strands displaying low-resolution video reflections of venue crowds.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Real-Time Depth Video Downscaling:</strong> Downscaled live depth camera video feeds to match the exact low-resolution pixel grid of hanging LED strands.</li><li><strong>Spatial Light Mapping:</strong> Mapped 3D string coordinates ensuring visual reflections maintain correct volumetric depth proportions as visitors move past.</li><li><strong>High-Refresh-Rate Art-Net Streaming:</strong> Streamed 60 FPS sACN lighting frames to custom field controllers for fluid motion responsiveness.</li></ul>
