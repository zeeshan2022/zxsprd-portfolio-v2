<h2>Interactive Smart Mirror Virtual Try-On Module</h2><p>Architected interactive retail smart mirrors tracking customer facial features to render real-time 3D virtual try-ons of eyewear and accessories.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Real-Time 3D Pose Estimation:</strong> Tracked customer head orientation and facial features to position virtual 3D eyewear models accurately.</li><li><strong>Dynamic Lighting Match Shaders:</strong> Adjusted virtual accessory lighting and reflection maps dynamically to match the physical store ambient light.</li><li><strong>Instant Social Sharing & Purchase Links:</strong> Generated instant mobile QR codes allowing customers to share virtual try-on photos and buy items online.</li></ul>
