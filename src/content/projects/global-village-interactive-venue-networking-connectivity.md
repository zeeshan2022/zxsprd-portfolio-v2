<h2>Global Village Interactive Venue Networking & Connectivity</h2><p>Architected robust outdoor networking backbones to handle high-density visitor traffic and power live interactive exhibits at Global Village.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>High-Density RF Tuning:</strong> Conducted outdoor spectrum planning and channel optimization across high-capacity wireless Access Points.</li><li><strong>Exhibit Dynamic VLAN Isolation:</strong> Segmented interactive exhibit displays, guest Wi-Fi, and POS payment terminals into isolated network subnets.</li><li><strong>Redundant Fiber Backbone:</strong> Laid ring-topology fiber optic backhaul lines ensuring zero downtime during high-concurrency event hours.</li></ul>
