<h2>Enterprise Privilege Identity Management (PIM) Approval Workflows</h2><p>Eliminated permanent standing administrative privileges across cloud tenancies by enforcing Just-In-Time role activation.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Just-In-Time Role Activation:</strong> Restricted Global Admin and Exchange Admin role activations to maximum 8-hour time-bound windows.</li><li><strong>Mandatory MFA & Ticket Reason:</strong> Required step-up MFA verification and valid IT service desk ticket numbers prior to role approval.</li><li><strong>Multi-Party Escalation Approval:</strong> Configured designated IT Manager approval requirements for sensitive domain-level role requests.</li></ul>
