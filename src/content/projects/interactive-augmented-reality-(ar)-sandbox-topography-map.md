<h2>Interactive Augmented Reality (AR) Sandbox Topography Map</h2><p>Architected educational AR sandbox installations reading sand elevation changes in real-time to project dynamic contour lines and simulated water flows.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Real-Time Depth Mesh Scanning:</strong> Calibrated overhead 3D cameras scanning physical sand height changes 30 times per second.</li><li><strong>Dynamic Topographic Color Shaders:</strong> Rendered instant color-coded elevation bands (blue water, green valleys, red peaks) matching sand contours.</li><li><strong>Simulated Water Physics:</strong> Executed fluid dynamics simulations allowing virtual rain to flow naturally down hand-carved sand valleys.</li></ul>
