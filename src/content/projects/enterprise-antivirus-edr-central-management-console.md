<h2>Enterprise Antivirus & EDR Central Management Console</h2><p>Implemented a centralized endpoint security management console to manage, monitor, and enforce antivirus and EDR policies across all enterprise assets.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Centralized Policy Push:</strong> Configured automated policy assignment rules based on Active Directory Organizational Unit (OU) membership.</li><li><strong>Automated Definition Staging:</strong> Built internal update server mirrors to distribute signature updates without saturating internet links.</li><li><strong>Real-Time Quarantine & Isolation:</strong> Configured automated endpoint network isolation rules upon detection of zero-day malware threats.</li></ul>
