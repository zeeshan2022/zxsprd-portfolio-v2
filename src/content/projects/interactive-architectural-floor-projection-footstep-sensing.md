<h2>Interactive Architectural Floor Projection & Footstep Sensing</h2><p>Architected architectural floor projection corridors generating real-time interactive light pools beneath the feet of walking guests.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Overhead Infrared Motion Tracking:</strong> Processed continuous overhead IR camera feeds isolating individual visitor footstep contact points.</li><li><strong>Low-Latency Dynamic Ripple Shaders:</strong> Rendered 60 FPS visual ripple and particle effects directly beneath tracked footstep coordinates.</li><li><strong>Automatic Ambient Light Compensation:</strong> Adjusted camera contrast thresholds automatically to maintain tracking accuracy under changing venue lighting.</li></ul>
