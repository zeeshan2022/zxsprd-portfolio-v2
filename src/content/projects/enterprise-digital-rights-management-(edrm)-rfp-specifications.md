<h2>Enterprise Digital Rights Management (EDRM) RFP Specifications</h2><p>Authored formal technical requirements for deploying enterprise-wide document classification, dynamic watermarking, and digital rights tracking.</p><h3>Key Features & Implementation</h3><ul><li><strong>Granular Rights Enforcement:</strong> Defined specifications restricting file printing, screen captures, editing, and forwarding for sensitive documents.</li><li><strong>Dynamic Watermarking:</strong> Mandated dynamic user-IP and timestamp watermarks on opened corporate attachments.</li><li><strong>M365 & Email Integration:</strong> Detailed seamless integration with Outlook DLP and local file servers.</li></ul>
